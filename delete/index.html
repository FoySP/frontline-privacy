Delete your Project Frontline account

Project Frontline (package com.frontline.game) is published by Steve Foyston. You can play without an account; this page is for players who created an online account (an automatic guest account, or a Google sign-in via Google Play Games).

Option 1: in the app

Open Settings, then Account, then Delete online account. This deletes your account on our server straight away.

Option 2: by email

Email stevefoyston@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Frontline account" and include:

  • your player id (starts with p_), if you can see it; and
  • whether you signed in with Google (do not send your password or any login code; we do not need them).

We may ask you to confirm by signing in. We aim to delete the account within 30 days and will reply when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your guest id, the link to your Google account, and your device mapping.
  • Your cloud save, its earlier versions and any archived saves.
  • Your arena entry and defence squad, rank and leaderboard data, event scores, party and friend entries.

What may remain for a limited time

  • Battle history entries in other players' logs may keep your old display name.
  • Server access logs (IP address, request path and status) and nightly backups, until they expire (a limited time, aiming for no more than 30 days).

Uninstalling the game removes local data on your phone only; it does not delete your server account. Local-only players who never went online have nothing on our server.
